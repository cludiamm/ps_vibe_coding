## What the evidence says

_What real usage showed: numbers if your tool has analytics, counted behaviour if it does not. Put the signal that matters on screen._

- **Primary signal:** The current analytics show promising engagement, but they do not yet prove that the guided checklist improves activation. The closest leading indicators are:  5 views per visit 3 minutes 45 seconds average duration These suggest users are exploring the product rather than immediately abandoning it.
- **What moved:** 20 visitors reached the prototype.
63 total page views were recorded.
Users explored approximately 5 screens per visit.
Average session duration was 3 minutes 45 seconds.
Approximately 55% of visitors continued beyond the first screen, based on the 45% bounce rate.
This supports the idea that the prototype is holding attention long enough for users to investigate multiple parts of the setup experience.
- **What didn't:** We cannot tell how many users selected a physical or virtual card.
We cannot tell how many invited teammates.
We cannot tell how many saved a spend policy.
We cannot tell how many reached or completed a first swipe.
There is no baseline or comparison group, so we cannot say whether the checklist improved activation versus the previous experience.
Also, 45% bounce rate is still a meaningful drop-off: nearly half of visitors left after one screen. That suggests the first screen or initial value proposition may still need improvement.

_Analytics snapshot: visitors 20; page views 63; views per visit 5; duration 3m 45 s; bounce 45%._
